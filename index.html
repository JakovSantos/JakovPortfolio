About Me

Hello World! I'm Jakov, an Information Technology student at Angeles University Foundation specializing in Web and Mobile Technologies. I chose this program because it sits right at the intersection of science, technology, and creativity, the three things I care about most. IT gave me a way to combine all three instead of picking just one.

On the technical side, I'm constantly learning and refining my craft, building applications that are functional, clean, and actually useful for the people who use them. But the creative side is just as much a part of who I am. I've always loved graphic design, digital drawing, and video editing, anything that lets me bring an idea to life visually. That creative instinct doesn't stay separate from my coding either. It shows up in how I think about interfaces, how interactions should feel, and how a user experiences a product from start to finish.

For me, good development isn't just about writing code that works. It's about building something that looks good, feels right, and tells a story, which is exactly why I've stayed in this field.
